    3) Historical CO2 Records from the Law Dome DE08, DE08-2, and DSS Ice Cores (1006 A.D.-1978 A.D)
        - co2_pre_1958.csv

DATA DESCRIPTION 

    1) The data source and format are provided extensively in the 'metadata.txt', 'sources.txt', 'stations.txt' and 'elements.txt' files of this folder. The relevant fields are the dates as well as the minimum daily temperatures reported as 10 times the Celcius degree.

    2) The data consist of monthly mean CO2 atmospheric concentrations in parts per million (ppm).

    3) The data consist of yearly mean CO2 atmospheric concentrations in parts per million (ppm).

All pre-processing steps are detailed and performed in the 'Case_study_part1.R' and 'Case_study_part2.R' files.
